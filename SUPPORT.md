# Support

This repository publishes the LCV Ideas & Software organization profile, the static organization
site, the sponsor page, and the default community-health files. It is not a product.

- Questions about a product or tool: open a Discussion or an issue in that product's repository, listed
  in the [organization repositories](https://github.com/orgs/LCV-Ideas-Software/repositories).
- Questions about this repository (profile, site, community-health defaults): open a
  [Discussion in `.github`](https://github.com/LCV-Ideas-Software/.github/discussions).
- Security vulnerabilities: do not open a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
- Commercial or institutional contact: <contato@lcv.dev>.

Support is provided on a best-effort basis by the maintainer. There is no service-level commitment.
